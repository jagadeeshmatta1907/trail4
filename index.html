📖 How to Play "SHOW"

The 3 to 8 player card passing & fast reaction party game

1
Scalable Cards Dealt (4 Cards Each):

Total cards = Players × 4 (e.g. 4 players = 16 cards, 5 players = 20 cards, 6 players = 24 cards). Each player secretly receives 4 cards.

2
Pass Cards Clockwise:

Each turn, choose 1 card from your 4 cards to pass to the next player. Aim to collect 4 cards of the exact same name!

3
Complete 4-of-a-Kind & Call SHOW:

As soon as all your 4 cards match, hit SUBMIT! This awards you 1st Place points and triggers an emergency alarm for everyone else.

4
The "SHOW!" Reaction Rush:

When "SHOW!" flashes on everyone's screen, all other players must press SUBMIT as fast as lightning! Faster reaction time = Higher place and more points.

5
Host-Customized Scoring & Rounds:

The host configures points for every rank and the number of rounds (e.g., 5 or 7 rounds). The player with the highest total points wins the Tournament Championship!
